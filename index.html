Our Story

In 2022, OpenAI released ChatGPT, making powerful AI available for pennies for anyone to use.
Now we hurtle toward the future at speed. It's terrifying, but it also gives each of us the power to shape the world.
Or so I hope. I'm Christopher Smith, and I founded Promptly to build AI-powered software for small businesses and nonprofits.

Scribert, your AI speech-to-text companion, transcribes your voice notes and outputs polished, formatted text. Try it free today!

Sign up
We recently launched Scribert, a subscription audio transcription app.
We also publish a free newsletter on how to use AI tools, A Knowledge Worker's Guide to the Singularity.
And we offer corporate trainings and educational seminars on AI workflows online and in person in the Albany, NY Capital Region.
At Promptly, we're working toward a future in which AI serves the cause of human thriving.
This requires new business and training paradigms that give workers equity ownership of models that train on their work.
Contact us today to discuss how you can support this mission. Let's partner to empower small businesses and non-profits through AI!
